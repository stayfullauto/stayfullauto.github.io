so,wegoalltheway

full auto is a studio that goes all the way. no half measures, nothing hidden.

we design and build apps, brands and websites, and we finish what we start. everything we make does exactly what it says and nothing behind your back: no trackers, no dark patterns, no data taken that isn't needed. fully automatic means one team from the first sketch to the final release, and still there after it ships.

workapps we built and use every day

what we do

  • product designone idea per screen. if a button doesn't earn its place, it goes.
  • android appsnative and offline first, with no analytics or ad sdks. we ask for the fewest permissions the app can run on, and say why in plain words.
  • brand identitya name, a wordmark and a look that holds from the app icon to a t-shirt.
  • websitesa page that says what the app does in plain words. no dark patterns, no cookie walls.

all the way through

  1. talk

    tell us the idea. we cut it down to the one thing it has to do.

  2. design

    screens you can tap through in a browser, changed until they're right.

  3. build

    a real app on your phone in days. every change is a build you can install.

  4. ship

    store listing, a privacy policy in plain words, and the release. then we keep it running.

does what it says.

if the store page says it, the app does it. if it doesn't say it, the app doesn't do it.

your data stays put.
